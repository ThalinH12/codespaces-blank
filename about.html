Our Story

Founded in 2018, North Star Bakery started as a small stand at our local farmers market. Our goal was simple: bring authentic, slow-fermented artisan bread back to our community tables.

Sourcing Philosophy

We source 100% of our flour from regional stone mills and partner with nearby dairy farms for non-GMO butter and milk. Every ingredient is chosen for sustainable farming practices and maximum flavor.
