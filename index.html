Welcome to North Star Bakery

Welcome to North Star Bakery! We are your local community bakery dedicated to crafting fresh, hand-made sourdough breads, pastries, and treats daily using organic ingredients.

We take pride in our slow-fermented artisan sourdoughs, flakey morning croissants, and custom celebration cakes made from locally sourced grains.
